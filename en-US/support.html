How the board, the practice sessions and the comparisons work.

Recording a dart

Throw a real dart at your real board, then touch the drawn board where it landed. Hold the finger and a readout names the bed under it (T20, D16, 25, Bull); lift to place the dart. The board follows the official geometry, so each touch is stored in millimetres from the centre. Use the Bounce-out button for a dart that did not stay in, and Take back if the finger slipped.

The figures

In the bed: the share of darts that landed in the bed you chose. Grouping: how far the darts sit from their own centre on average, in millimetres; smaller is tighter. Mean miss: the average distance from the centre of the bed. Mean score: the score a dart would have taken, averaged. A line under the figures says which way the group leans.

Comparisons

Pick two setups that differ in one thing (the bench checks the specs), one bed, the darts per block, the blocks per day and a warm-up. Blocks run in ABBA order so warming up and tiring out fall on both setups alike. Throw another day and the order is reversed. The report shows both setups on one board with the figures side by side. Its last sentence tells you whether the difference is bigger than chance would give for that many darts; it is descriptive, not a statistical test.

Why one difference

If two setups differ in weight and flights at once, a difference on the board cannot be pinned on either. The editor warns when more than one spec differs; you can still lock the comparison, but the report will be about the pair, not the part.

Backups

Settings › Back up the record writes one JSON file you can keep anywhere; Restore reads it back. The CSV of a comparison (Pro) has one row per dart: day, block, condition, ring, sector, score, position in millimetres, bounce-out and comfort.

My finger lands a little off where the dart went.

Hold the finger down and read the bed name before lifting; move until it says the right bed. For fine placement, the readout is the thing to trust.

Can the app score 501 or cricket?

No. DartBench measures practice: where darts land and how tightly they group. A scoring app is a different tool.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Open the Pro page or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
